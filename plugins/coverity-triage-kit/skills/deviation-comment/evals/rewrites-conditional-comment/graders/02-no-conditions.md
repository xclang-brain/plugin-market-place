The rewritten deviation comment is unconditional. It has no condition, caveat, guess, request or instruction to the reader: none of ただし, 但し, 場合に限, 場合のみ, に限り, 場合は, 前提, に依存, 間だけ, 見直, おそらく, と思われ, ください, を推奨, すべき, and no sentence that tells the reader what to do (for example one ending in 〜こと。). A stated precondition such as 「この判断の前提: …」 fails even if none of these words is used.
